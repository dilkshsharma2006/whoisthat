## 1. Read of the Game

The dossier's central conclusion — that survival is an unbreakable ~60 s wall and score is therefore a pure damage-throughput problem inside 60 s — is **false**, because the experiment that "proved" it was invalidated by the dossier's own bug.

Look at the §2.1 survival table alongside §7.5:

* The "true pacifist (never fire, never hunt)" bot averaged **1.19 kills** and died at 56 s; the "coward (no fire, no hunt, kite always)" bot averaged **1.31 kills** and died at 58 s. A bot that never fires cannot average 1.2–1.3 kills.


* §7.5 explains why: the firing branches pass literal `fire=True` and `fire_gate` only scales utility, so **the bot never stopped shooting**.


* Continuous accidental firing destroys survival in two ways simultaneously: every shot broadcasts a 320–420 m sound beacon to a shared-vision squad, and **dealing damage resets your own 5-second HP regen timer** just like taking damage.



Worse, the current loop sabotages its own kills and survival at the exact same time:

* **Step 11 overrides Step 8 on a single scratch:** `Hunt` closes to 45 m at >45% HP, then taking one 45-damage bullet drops HP below 97%, triggering a 6-second boost-flee (`kite`). That aborts winnable 1v1 duels mid-kill (producing the 4,463 damage / 0 kills trace), lets the wounded enemy regen at 45 HP/s, burns all energy, and still fires stray shots that block *your* regen.


* **The bot dies 1 second before the game actually starts:** Phase 1 zone shrink begins at `t = 60 s` and Supply Drop 1 lands at `t = 95 s`. Dying at 59 s means you fight on a single 1,400 HP budget, collect zero supply-drop full heals, and collect zero zone attrition.


* **The laser throughput calculation in §0.6/§2.1 is wrong:** laser cycle time is `charge (0.75 s) + cooldown (1.65 s) = 2.40 s`, giving **131.25 dps**, not 190 dps. Even with gun interleaving (~220–240 dps peak), killing 5.3 bots (~7,400+ HP) across a 1067 × 722 m map with 130 m minimum spawn separation inside 60 s against escalating squad fire is physically impossible.



**Resolution of the central puzzle:** The 5.96 leader is **not** killing 5 bots in 60 seconds, nor passively camping to 5.96. They are playing **multi-life attrition over 180–300+ seconds**: breaking the single-life 1,400 HP trap into 3–5 renewable health bars via true silent regen cycles (`45 HP/s`, or up to `115.2 HP/s` after `2.0 s` with 2× `nanites`), rooftop/building LOS breaks (ignoring `jump` + `jet` in a 3D campus arena throws away 25% of loot and instant parapet occlusion), and Supply Drops (`t = 95 / 165 / 235 s` full HP + energy + 3 cubes). Surviving 3–4× longer lets you win 4–5 isolated, snowballed duels while environmental attrition (NPC drones + zone shrink) adds ~1.5–2.0 deaths. Local proxy bias (§13: hunt v1 was flat locally, +0.61 officially) suppresses local scores further, making **survival time, HP regenerated, damage-per-kill, and kills** better local diagnostics than raw local score.

**What the dossier is optimising vs. the right objective:**
The dossier optimises **raw DPS inside a fixed 60 s life**. The true objective is:


$$\mathbb{E}[\text{score}] = \underbrace{N_{\text{cycles}}(T_{\text{surv}}) \cdot P(\text{kill} \mid \text{duel})}_{\text{snowballed kills}} + \underbrace{\text{EnvDeaths}(T_{\text{surv}})}_{\text{NPCs + zone}}$$


This is governed by three coupled quantities: (1) **Effective HP lifespan** (real fire-suppressed regen cycles + rooftops + drops), (2) **Kill conversion** (committing to finish isolated targets instead of chipping and fleeing at 97% HP), and (3) **Asymmetric burst/trading** (weapon acquisition + `memory_s` peek-charging + verified-arc mortar).

*Note on the "never fight each other" loophole:* Because the engine simulates a standard 16-mech FFA with no team-damage flag in `hello.config`, enemy projectiles/mortars can hit squadmates via body-blocking or splash, and NPC drones aggro any mech within 45 m. Treat body-blocking, mortar knockback into late zones, and opportunistic drone dragging as tactical bonuses (~0.2–0.5 score), not the core engine.

## 2. Policy Specification

### 2.1 Architecture, I/O, and Budget

* Init (`hello`, ≤20 s budget): Parse all parameters dynamically from `hello.config` (zero hard-coded physics/weapon/zone/upgrade constants). Build `World` (or `NullWorld` if `/arena` is unavailable), 8 m `NavGrid`, and precompute `RooftopIndex` (all climbable building prisms with footprint area ≥ 64 m², height above local ground ∈ `[5.0, 0.75 * max_jet_climb]`, climb entry points, and parapet peek points). Init time: ~250 ms.


* Per-tick input (`obs`, 10 Hz, 50 ms cap): `tick`, `t`, `alive`, `self`, `mechs`, `projectiles`, `crates`, `cubes`, `pickups`, `sounds`, `beams`, `events`, `drops`, `zone`, `offer`.


* **Per-tick output (`action`):** `{"tick", "move", "boost", "jump", "aim", "fire", "reload", "pick?", "take?", "drop?"}`.


* **Complexity:** `O(M + P + R_near)` per tick (`M ≤ 16` mechs, `P` projectiles, `R_near ≤ 16` nearby roofs) plus bounded A* (`≤ 2.2 ms` cap, replanned only when goal moves `> 15 m` or age `> 2.5 s`). Total runtime: **~2 ms typical, < 8 ms p99** on 1 CPU.

### 2.3 Exact Config-Derived Quantities

All thresholds read live from `hello.config` and `self.stats`:

* `step_quiet = max(0.1, (cfg["sound"]["step_speed"] - 0.25) / self.stats["walk_speed"])` (silent walk fraction).


* `max_jet_climb = (self.stats["energy"] / self.stats["jet_drain"]) * self.stats["max_rise_speed"] * 0.85` (safe climb ceiling).


* `current_zone(t)`: linear interpolation of `(center, radius)` between `shrink_start` and `shrink_end`.


* `dmg_mult = 1.0 + cfg["cubes"]["damage_per_cube"] * min(self.cubes, cfg["cubes"]["stat_cap"])`.


* `outnumbered`: count of enemy mechs within `125 m` that either have LOS to us, damaged us within `3.0 s`, or are closing toward us inside `90 m`.

### 2.4 Seven-State Hierarchical FSM

Evaluated top-to-bottom every tick; the first matching guard owns movement and firing policy.

#### 1. `ZONE_EMERGENCY`

* **Enter:** `not self.in_zone`, OR distance to `zone.next_center` exceeds `zone.next_radius - 12` AND time required to walk there at `0.85 * walk_speed` exceeds `(zone.shrink_end - t) - 6.0`.


* **Exit:** Inside `next_radius - 20` and `self.in_zone`.
* **Action:** Move via A* toward the closest safe point inside `next_radius - 25` (with route clearance penalty against enemies). Boost if outside zone or behind shrink clock. **Fire policy:** `DEFENSIVE_ONLY` (shoot only targets within 70 m that have LOS and are blocking/damaging us; suppress fire if `hp_frac < 0.45` and outside LOS to protect regen).

#### 2. `RECOVER` (True Multi-Life Heal Cycle — Hard Fire Suppression)

* **Enter:**
* `hp_frac < recover_enter_hp` (Early: `0.52`, Mid: `0.58`, Late: `0.48`) AND no enemy is immediately finishable (`enemy_hp <= lethal_threshold`, see §2.5), **OR**
* `outnumbered >= 2` AND `hp_frac < 0.80` AND no enemy is finishable within `1.5 s`.


* **Exit:**
* `hp_frac >= recover_exit_hp` (Early/Mid: `0.88`, Late: `0.78`), **OR**
* Emergency close-quarters survival: an enemy with LOS is `< 28 m` away and closing, and `hp_frac > 0.22` (fight back rather than die silently), **OR**
* `t - state_since > 18.0 s` without starting regen (break stalemate by relocating).


* **Movement:**
1. If on a rooftop (`World` available), step `3.5–5.0 m` back from the edge away from the threat centroid to break ground LOS immediately (zero energy spent).
2. Otherwise, pick the best **occlusion destination** within 65 m: either a climbable rooftop (if `energy >= 45` and climb height `<= 28 m`) or a hard building corner where `shot_clear(dest, enemy)` is `False` for all known threats within 150 m.
3. Boost (`boost=True`) **only until LOS is broken** (or `energy < 20`), then drop to `step_quiet` so footsteps stay silent (`< 40 m` sound radius).




* **Torso & Fire Policy (CRITICAL):**
* **`fire = [False, False]` HARD-SUPPRESSED.** Zero exceptions while in `RECOVER`. Never poke, never shoot crates/NPCs, never charge laser. This guarantees `last_combat_t` stops resetting and `regen_delay` completes.


* Point torso `aim[0]` toward the threat centroid (or latest `damaged.bearing`) so all incoming shots hit the 180° front arc (preventing ×1.5 rear damage).





#### 3. `DROP_CONTEST`

* **Enter:** Any drop in `obs.drops` satisfies:
* `lands_in <= 22.0 s` and travel time at walk speed `<= lands_in + 8.0 s`,


* drop lies inside `next_zone`,
* `hp_frac < 0.78` OR `self.cubes < stat_cap`,


* fewer than 2 enemies are visibly within 55 m of the drop (unless `holder is not None` and `enemies_near == 1` and `hp_frac > 0.60`, in which case ambush the stationary captor).


* **Exit:** Drop disappears, `enemies_near >= 2` while `hp_frac < 0.65`, or `RECOVER` triggers.
* **Action:** Approach quietly (`step_quiet` if `lands_in > 4 s` and safe), enter the `radius` (8 m) ring at `lands_in <= 1.5 s`, strafe inside the ring while facing the highest-probability approach vector. Do not fire at distant enemies (>80 m) during the 4 s capture clock unless fired upon.



#### 4. `DUEL_FINISH` (Committed 1v1 Execution — No 97%-HP Abort)

* **Enter:**
* A visible/tracked enemy (`t - last_seen_t <= 2.5 s`) exists within engage range (`160 m` with laser/mortar, `115 m` with gun only), AND
* `outnumbered <= 1` (or the target is finishable: `hp <= max(380, 1.35 * best_burst)`), AND
* `hp_frac >= engage_hp` (Early: `0.58`, Mid: `0.62`, Late: `0.48`), AND
* Target is not currently being ignored for a higher-priority free weapon within 20 m (`t < 30 s` and no secondary weapon).


* **Target Lock & Hysteresis:**
* Score each candidate enemy: `base = (1.0 - hp/max_hp)*120 + (45 if isolated else 0) + (35 if holds_mortar and we_have_laser else 0) + (25 if holds_laser and we_have_lightning and dist<55 else 0) - 0.35*dist`.


* Keep `lock_id` until: target dies, target loses LOS for `> 3.5 s` with no indirect mortar arc, or another target is `< 280 HP` and within LOS.


* **Combat Movement (Weapon-Specific):**
* **Laser Peek-Charge (when near corner/roof edge and `dist ∈ [45, 240]`):** Pull behind cover (`shot_clear == False`), pre-aim at `belief.pos` (real-time via `memory_s = 5 s`), hold `fire[laser] = True`. When `charge >= 0.65 * charge_max` (or `>= lethal_charge - 0.10`), step out to the peek point, release on the first tick with clear LOS and aim error `< 0.85 * enemy_radius`, then step back into cover during the `1.65 s` cooldown.


* **Open Duel (Gun + Secondary):** Maintain preferred standoff (`55–95 m` with Gun+Laser, `65–120 m` with Gun+Mortar, `22–38 m` with Lightning finisher, `45–70 m` Gun-only). Strafe perpendicular to enemy aim at full `walk_speed` (never boost-waste energy unless dodging mortar/laser or closing on a `< 350 HP` fleeing target). Keep an occluder within 15 m on our flank for instant `RECOVER` transition.
* **Close-Range Roof Defense:** If holding a roof and an enemy jets upward within 40 m, step to the edge and focus them mid-air (and lob mortar to knock them off).




* **Fire Policy:** `COMMITTED` — fire every valid weapon through the single-torso Arbiter (§2.5 & §3.1) **at `lock_id` only** (exception: lightning chain / mortar cluster splash hitting `lock_id` + neighbors, or an enemy `< 35 m` directly blocking us). Never waste shots poking unrelated distant bots.

#### 5. `WEAPON_ACQUIRE`

* **Enter:** Slot 1 is empty (or holds `lightning` while a known `laser`/`mortar` is within `60 m` and we are outside late game), `t < 200 s`, and either a known floor pickup (`seen_pickups`) is within `140 m` inside the zone, or (`t < 40 s` and no secondary yet) we sweep toward the nearest rooftop/high-density campus cluster to spot floor spawns (`25%` sit on roofs).


* **Action:** Move silently (`step_quiet` when enemies heard/near, full walk otherwise — **never shoot crates** while searching so we don't ring a 220 m dinner bell). When within `loot.pickup_radius` (4 m horizontally & vertically), emit `take: id` (and `drop: 1` only if upgrading secondary and laser is not charging).



#### 6. `ROOF_CLIMB`

* **Enter:** `World` is loaded, we are NOT already on a roof, `energy >= 55`, a precomputed rooftop inside `next_zone` lies within `55 m`, its climb height is `<= 0.6 * max_jet_climb`, and no enemy has clear LOS on the climb wall within `70 m` (or we are climbing to break LOS for `RECOVER`).
* **Action:** Executed by `RoofClimber` (§3.3): navigate to clear climb entry point → `jump=True` → airborne `boost=True` until `self.pos[2] >= roof_z + 0.35` → move horizontally onto roof interior → `boost=False`. Abort climb if damaged during ascent and `hp_frac < 0.40` (drop behind ground wall instead).



#### 7. `STALK_ROTATE` (Default)

* **Action:** Rotate early into `next_zone` toward a rooftop or high-cover anchor biased away from loud sound sectors and NPC leashes. Walk silently (`step_quiet`) whenever no enemy is visible. Detour ≤ 22 m for cubes **only if** `self.cubes < stat_cap` OR `(next_upgrade_threshold - self.cubes) <= 2` (avoiding uncapped `+5%` hitbox bloat past 10 cubes). Post-kill cube sweep: for `6.0 s` after a `kill` event, sprint to the kill site to grab the 3 dropped cubes if no enemy is within `55 m`. Torso scans `±0.65 rad` biased toward recent sound bearings.



### 2.5 Weapon Priority, Loadout, and Upgrade Valuation

* **Slot 0:** Always retain `gun` in Early/Mid game (zero energy cost, reliable during secondary cooldowns, never respawns on floor or corpses). Swap Slot 0 to `lightning` **only** in Late game (`zone.radius <= 90` and we already hold `laser` in Slot 1).


* **Slot 1 Ranking:**
* Early/Mid (`zone.radius > 100`): `laser > mortar > lightning`.
* Late (`zone.radius <= 100` or `kills >= 4`): `mortar ≈ laser > lightning` (mortar splash punishes late squad clustering and knocks bots out of the tiny ring).




* **Arbiter & Firing Rules:**
* Only one torso aim is commanded per tick. Pick highest-utility firing candidate; fire secondary slot concurrently only if its `shot_error` along that **same** torso aim is inside tolerance (or inside lightning's `cone_deg`).


* **Hard Gate:** If the FSM sets `fire_mode == "SUPPRESS"`, return `fire = [False, False]` directly — zero bypasses.
* **Laser Lethal Release:** Release charge as soon as `charge >= lethal_charge(target_hp)` (never over-charge for 0.75 s to kill a 150 HP enemy) and `shot_clear` is True along current `self.aim`.


* **Verified-Arc Mortar:** Solve both low and high ballistic trajectories with 2-step target-velocity lead; sample 24 points along the parabola against `world.solid`; allow indirect fire **only** when the parabola clears every prism and ground cell (fixes the −0.28 wall-clipping failure in §9.2). Bonus utility `+120` per additional enemy within `splash_radius`.


* **Early Gun Reload:** Send `reload=True` whenever `ammo < 5` and either LOS is broken or we are in `RECOVER`/`STALK_ROTATE`.




* **Upgrade Valuation (`pick`):** Evaluate marginal value dynamically from `hello.config.upgrades`, weighted for **Multi-Life + TTK**:


* **Tier S (weight 100):** `nanites` (1st stack: `3.5 s / 72 HP/s`; 2nd stack: `2.0 s / 115.2 HP/s` — turns every 5 s break into a full reset).


* **Tier A (weight 75–85):** `laser_damage`, `laser_charge`, `gun_damage`, `gun_rate`, `mortar_reload`, `mortar_splash` (shrinks TTK before squad reinforcements arrive).


* **Tier A− (weight 65–72):** `armor`, `plating` (widens duel HP margin).


* **Tier B (weight 45–55):** `gun_mag`, `gun_velocity`, `servos`, `jets`, `reactor`, `lightning_efficiency`, `lightning_range`.
* **Tier C (weight 20–35):** `capacitor`, `gyros`, `sensors`, `mortar_velocity`, `laser_pierce`, `lightning_chain`.

### 3.1 Combat Clock, Verified-Arc Mortar, Lethal-Release Laser & Hard-Gated Arbiter

```python
import math
from dataclasses import dataclass
from typing import Any, Optional

def wrap_angle(a: float) -> float:
    return (a + math.pi) % (2.0 * math.pi) - math.pi

def clamp(x: float, lo: float, hi: float) -> float:
    return lo if x < lo else (hi if x > hi else x)

def dir_from_aim(yaw: float, pitch: float) -> tuple[float, float, float]:
    cp = math.cos(pitch)
    return (cp * math.cos(yaw), cp * math.sin(yaw), math.sin(pitch))

def aim_to_point(src: tuple[float, float, float], dst: tuple[float, float, float]) -> tuple[float, float]:
    dx, dy, dz = dst[0] - src[0], dst[1] - src[1], dst[2] - src[2]
    horiz = math.hypot(dx, dy)
    return (math.atan2(dy, dx), math.atan2(dz, max(1e-6, horiz)))

def shot_perp_error(
    src: tuple[float, float, float],
    dst: tuple[float, float, float],
    yaw: float,
    pitch: float,
) -> float:
    vx, vy, vz = dst[0] - src[0], dst[1] - src[1], dst[2] - src[2]
    ax, ay, az = dir_from_aim(yaw, pitch)
    proj = vx * ax + vy * ay + vz * az
    if proj <= 0.0:
        return 1e9
    cx, cy, cz = vx - proj * ax, vy - proj * ay, vz - proj * az
    return math.sqrt(cx * cx + cy * cy + cz * cz)

def slew_aim(
    cur: tuple[float, float],
    cmd: tuple[float, float],
    turn_rate: float,
    pitch_rate: float,
    min_p: float,
    max_p: float,
    dt: float,
) -> tuple[float, float]:
    dy = wrap_angle(cmd[0] - cur[0])
    dp = clamp(cmd[1], min_p, max_p) - cur[1]
    ny = wrap_angle(cur[0] + clamp(dy, -turn_rate * dt, turn_rate * dt))
    np = clamp(cur[1] + clamp(dp, -pitch_rate * dt, pitch_rate * dt), min_p, max_p)
    return (ny, np)


class CombatClock:
    """Tracks exact out-of-combat regen readiness from protocol events."""
    def __init__(self, cfg: dict[str, Any]):
        self.cfg = cfg
        self.last_combat_t: float = -999.0
        self.prev_hp: float = cfg["mech"]["hp"]
        self.kills: int = 0
        self.last_kill_t: float = -999.0
        self.last_kill_id: Optional[int] = None
        self.last_damaged_bearing: Optional[float] = None
        self.last_damaged_t: float = -999.0

    def update(self, obs: dict[str, Any]) -> None:
        t = obs["t"]
        me = obs["self"]
        if not me["in_zone"] or me["hp"] < self.prev_hp - 1e-3:
            self.last_combat_t = t
        for ev in obs.get("events", ()):
            et = ev.get("type")
            if et == "damaged":
                self.last_combat_t = t
                if ev.get("bearing") is not None:
                    self.last_damaged_bearing = ev["bearing"]
                    self.last_damaged_t = t
            elif et == "hit":
                # Dealing damage to mech/NPC/crate resets regen timer.
                self.last_combat_t = t
            elif et == "kill":
                self.kills += 1
                self.last_kill_t = t
                self.last_kill_id = ev.get("target")
        self.prev_hp = me["hp"]

    def time_to_regen(self, obs: dict[str, Any]) -> float:
        delay = obs["self"]["stats"]["regen_delay"]
        return max(0.0, delay - (obs["t"] - self.last_combat_t))

    def is_regenerating(self, obs: dict[str, Any]) -> bool:
        return obs["self"]["in_zone"] and self.time_to_regen(obs) <= 0.0


def solve_mortar_arc(
    src: tuple[float, float, float],
    tgt_pos: tuple[float, float, float],
    tgt_vel: tuple[float, float, float],
    speed: float,
    gravity: float,
    min_pitch: float,
    max_pitch: float,
    ttl: float,
    world: Any,
    prefer_high: bool = False,
) -> Optional[tuple[float, float, float, tuple[float, float, float]]]:
    """
    Two-pass moving-target ballistic solver with full parabola solid-collision check.
    Returns (yaw, pitch, flight_time, predicted_impact) or None if no valid clear arc.
    """
    pred = tgt_pos
    best_solution = None
    branches = (True, False) if prefer_high else (False, True)

    for high in branches:
        p = tgt_pos
        sol = None
        for _ in range(2):
            dx, dy = p[0] - src[0], p[1] - src[1]
            R = math.hypot(dx, dy)
            if R < 10.0:
                sol = None
                break
            dz = p[2] - src[2]
            v2 = speed * speed
            disc = v2 * v2 - gravity * (gravity * R * R + 2.0 * dz * v2)
            if disc < 0.0:
                sol = None
                break
            root = math.sqrt(disc)
            tan_th = (v2 + root) / (gravity * R) if high else (v2 - root) / (gravity * R)
            pitch = math.atan(tan_th)
            if pitch < min_pitch or pitch > max_pitch:
                sol = None
                break
            cp = math.cos(pitch)
            t_flight = R / max(1e-6, speed * cp)
            if t_flight > ttl:
                sol = None
                break
            yaw = math.atan2(dy, dx)
            # Lead target clamped slightly so we do not predict through solid walls
            lead_t = min(t_flight, 2.2)
            np = (
                tgt_pos[0] + tgt_vel[0] * lead_t,
                tgt_pos[1] + tgt_vel[1] * lead_t,
                tgt_pos[2],
            )
            if world is not None and hasattr(world, "solid") and world.solid(np[0], np[1], np[2] + 2.0):
                np = tgt_pos
            p = np
            sol = (yaw, pitch, t_flight, p)

        if sol is None:
            continue

        # Validate entire parabolic trajectory against solid prisms & terrain
        yaw, pitch, t_flight, imp = sol
        ax, ay, az = dir_from_aim(yaw, pitch)
        vx, vy, vz = speed * ax, speed * ay, speed * az
        clear = True
        if world is not None and hasattr(world, "solid"):
            steps = max(12, min(28, int(t_flight * 10)))
            for i in range(1, steps):
                tau = t_flight * (i / steps)
                x = src[0] + vx * tau
                y = src[1] + vy * tau
                z = src[2] + vz * tau - 0.5 * gravity * tau * tau
                if world.solid(x, y, z):
                    clear = False
                    break
        if clear:
            best_solution = sol
            break

    return best_solution

    is_laser_release: bool = False


class WeaponArbiter:
    """Single-torso arbiter with HARD fire suppression and lethal laser release."""
    def __init__(self, cfg: dict[str, Any]):
        self.cfg = cfg

    def damage_mult(self, cubes: int) -> float:
        c = self.cfg["cubes"]
        return 1.0 + c["damage_per_cube"] * min(cubes, c["stat_cap"])

    def muzzle_pos(self, me: dict[str, Any], is_mortar: bool = False) -> tuple[float, float, float]:
        x, y, z = me["pos"]
        st = me["stats"]
        if is_mortar:
            h = st.get("height", self.cfg["mech"]["height"]) + 0.5
        else:
            h = st.get("muzzle_height", self.cfg["mech"]["muzzle_height"])
        return (x, y, z + h)

    def _enemy_radius(self, enemy: dict[str, Any]) -> float:
        base_r = self.cfg["mech"]["radius"]
        spc = self.cfg["cubes"]["size_per_cube"]
        return base_r * (1.0 + spc * enemy.get("cubes", 0))

    def _enemy_mid(self, enemy: dict[str, Any]) -> tuple[float, float, float]:
        base_h = self.cfg["mech"]["height"]
        spc = self.cfg["cubes"]["size_per_cube"]
        h = base_h * (1.0 + spc * enemy.get("cubes", 0))
        x, y, z = enemy["pos"]
        return (x, y, z + 0.5 * h)

    def decide(
        self,
        obs: dict[str, Any],
        target: Optional[dict[str, Any]],
        all_enemies: list[dict[str, Any]],
        fire_mode: str,   # "SUPPRESS" | "DEFENSIVE" | "COMMITTED" | "PEEK_CHARGE"
        world: Any,
        fallback_aim: tuple[float, float],
    ) -> tuple[tuple[float, float], list[bool], bool]:
        me = obs["self"]
        st = me["stats"]
        cur_aim = (me["aim"][0], me["aim"][1])
        weapons = me["weapons"]

        # HARD SUPPRESSION: fixes the §7.5 fire_gate bug completely.
        if fire_mode == "SUPPRESS" or target is None:
            # Never start or hold fire; if laser was somehow mid-charge, let it release cleanly off-target.
            reload_cmd = self._want_reload(weapons, in_combat=False)
            return fallback_aim, [False, False], reload_cmd

        dmult = self.damage_mult(me["cubes"])
        tgt_mid = self._enemy_mid(target)
        tgt_r = self._enemy_radius(target)
        tgt_vel = target.get("vel", [0.0, 0.0, 0.0])
        tgt_hp = target.get("hp", self.cfg["mech"]["hp"])
        tgt_weapons = target.get("weapons", [])
        muzzle = self.muzzle_pos(me, is_mortar=False)
        dist = math.hypot(tgt_mid[0] - muzzle[0], tgt_mid[1] - muzzle[1])
        has_los = True if (world is None or not hasattr(world, "shot_clear")) else world.shot_clear(muzzle, tgt_mid)

        candidates: list[FireCandidate] = []
        turn_r = st["turn_rate"]
        pitch_r = st["pitch_rate"]
        min_p, max_p = st["min_pitch"], st["max_pitch"]

        for slot, w in enumerate(weapons):
            if not w:
                continue
            kind = w["kind"]
            ws = w["stats"]
            cd = w.get("cooldown", 0.0)

            if kind == "laser":
                charge = w.get("charge", 0.0)
                c_max = ws["charge"]
                c_min = ws["min_charge"]
                min_frac = ws["min_frac"]
                cook = (1.0 + ws.get("cookoff", 0.0)) if "mortar" in tgt_weapons else 1.0
                max_dmg = ws["damage"] * dmult * cook
                # Exact minimum charge required to kill this target right now
                req_frac = (tgt_hp * 1.06) / max(1.0, max_dmg)
                if req_frac <= 1.0:
                    lethal_c = max(c_min, c_max * max(0.0, (req_frac - min_frac) / max(1e-6, 1.0 - min_frac)))
                else:
                    lethal_c = c_max

                aim = aim_to_point(muzzle, tgt_mid)
                if charge > 0.0:
                    # Check if we can release hitscan beam right now along actual torso aim
                    err_now = shot_perp_error(muzzle, tgt_mid, cur_aim[0], cur_aim[1])
                    next_aim = slew_aim(cur_aim, aim, turn_r, pitch_r, min_p, max_p, 0.05)
                    err_tick0 = shot_perp_error(muzzle, tgt_mid, next_aim[0], next_aim[1])
                    ready_dmg = charge >= min(c_max * 0.92, lethal_c)
                    almost_auto = charge >= c_max + ws.get("hold_s", 2.0) - 0.25
                    if has_los and dist <= ws["range"] and (ready_dmg or almost_auto) and min(err_now, err_tick0) <= tgt_r * 0.90:
                        candidates.append(FireCandidate(
                            slot=slot, kind="laser", aim=aim, point=tgt_mid,
                            tol=tgt_r * 0.9, utility=220.0 * cook,
                            fire_flag=False, is_laser_release=True,
                        ))
                    else:
                        # Keep holding charge while slewing / peeking
                        candidates.append(FireCandidate(
                            slot=slot, kind="laser", aim=aim, point=tgt_mid,
                            tol=tgt_r, utility=180.0 * cook,
                            fire_flag=True, is_laser_release=False,
                        ))
                elif cd <= 0.15 and dist <= ws["range"]:
                    # Start charging if we have LOS OR are explicitly peek-charging behind cover
                    if has_los or fire_mode == "PEEK_CHARGE":
                        candidates.append(FireCandidate(
                            slot=slot, kind="laser", aim=aim, point=tgt_mid,
                            tol=tgt_r, utility=165.0 * cook,
                            fire_flag=True, is_laser_release=False,
                        ))

            elif kind == "lightning":
                if cd <= 0.05 and has_los and dist <= ws["range"] and me["energy"] >= ws["energy"] + 8:
                    aim = aim_to_point(muzzle, tgt_mid)
                    cone_rad = math.radians(ws["cone_deg"]) * 0.5
                    # Bonus against laser-holders (short effect) and clustered bots
                    chain_n = sum(
                        1 for e in all_enemies
                        if e["id"] != target["id"]
                        and math.hypot(e["pos"][0] - target["pos"][0], e["pos"][1] - target["pos"][1]) <= ws["chain_range"]
                    )
                    short_bonus = 55.0 if "laser" in tgt_weapons else 0.0
                    util = 145.0 + 45.0 * min(chain_n, ws["chain"]) + short_bonus
                    if abs(wrap_angle(aim[0] - cur_aim[0])) <= cone_rad:
                        candidates.append(FireCandidate(
                            slot=slot, kind="lightning", aim=aim, point=tgt_mid,
                            tol=cone_rad, utility=util, fire_flag=True,
                        ))
                    else:
                        # Slew toward lightning target without wasting energy yet
                        candidates.append(FireCandidate(
                            slot=slot, kind="lightning", aim=aim, point=tgt_mid,
                            tol=cone_rad, utility=util * 0.6, fire_flag=False,
                        ))

            elif kind == "mortar":
                if cd <= 0.15 and dist >= ws["splash_radius"] + 12.0:
                    m_src = self.muzzle_pos(me, is_mortar=True)
                    # Check cluster target: pick centroid if >=2 enemies within splash_radius
                    best_pt = (target["pos"][0], target["pos"][1], target["pos"][2] + 1.0)
                    cluster_count = 1
                    sr = ws["splash_radius"]
                    for e in all_enemies:
                        if e["id"] == target["id"]:
                            continue
                        if math.hypot(e["pos"][0] - target["pos"][0], e["pos"][1] - target["pos"][1]) <= sr * 1.4:
                            best_pt = (
                                0.5 * (target["pos"][0] + e["pos"][0]),
                                0.5 * (target["pos"][1] + e["pos"][1]),
                                0.5 * (target["pos"][2] + e["pos"][2]) + 1.0,
                            )
                            cluster_count += 1
                    sol = solve_mortar_arc(
                        m_src, best_pt, tgt_vel, ws["speed"], ws["gravity"],
                        min_p, max_p, ws["ttl"], world, prefer_high=(not has_los),
                    )
                    if sol is not None:
                        myaw, mpitch, t_flight, imp = sol
                        # Verify torso will align within 0.08 rad before firing
                        next_aim = slew_aim(cur_aim, (myaw, mpitch), turn_r, pitch_r, min_p, max_p, 0.1)
                        ang_err = math.hypot(wrap_angle(myaw - next_aim[0]), mpitch - next_aim[1])
                        util = (130.0 + 75.0 * (cluster_count - 1)) * (1.0 if t_flight < 3.5 else 0.7)
                        candidates.append(FireCandidate(
                            slot=slot, kind="mortar", aim=(myaw, mpitch), point=imp,
                            tol=0.07, utility=util, fire_flag=(ang_err <= 0.07),
                        ))

            elif kind == "gun":
                if cd <= 0.05 and w.get("reload", 0.0) <= 0.0 and w.get("ammo", 0) > 0:
                    if has_los and dist <= ws["range"]:
                        t_lead = dist / max(1.0, ws["speed"])
                        lead_pt = (
                            tgt_mid[0] + tgt_vel[0] * t_lead,
                            tgt_mid[1] + tgt_vel[1] * t_lead,
                            tgt_mid[2] + tgt_vel[2] * t_lead,
                        )
                        aim = aim_to_point(muzzle, lead_pt)
                        next_aim = slew_aim(cur_aim, aim, turn_r, pitch_r, min_p, max_p, 0.1)
                        err = shot_perp_error(muzzle, lead_pt, next_aim[0], next_aim[1])
                        tol = tgt_r * 1.15
                        candidates.append(FireCandidate(
                            slot=slot, kind="gun", aim=aim, point=lead_pt,
                            tol=tol, utility=95.0, fire_flag=(err <= tol),
                        ))

        if not candidates:
            aim = aim_to_point(muzzle, tgt_mid) if target else fallback_aim
            return aim, [False, False], self._want_reload(weapons, in_combat=has_los)

        # Pick single torso winner
        candidates.sort(key=lambda c: c.utility, reverse=True)
        winner = candidates[0]
        cmd_aim = winner.aim
        fire = [False, False]
        fire[winner.slot] = winner.fire_flag

        # Opportunistically fire the other slot ONLY if compatible with the same torso aim
        next_aim = slew_aim(cur_aim, cmd_aim, turn_r, pitch_r, min_p, max_p, 0.1)
        for c in candidates[1:]:
            if c.slot == winner.slot:
                continue
            if c.kind == "laser":
                # Keep laser charging if already charging; never accidentally release off-aim
                if weapons[c.slot] and weapons[c.slot].get("charge", 0.0) > 0.0:
                    fire[c.slot] = True
            elif c.kind == "lightning" and c.fire_flag:
                if abs(wrap_angle(c.aim[0] - cur_aim[0])) <= c.tol:
                    fire[c.slot] = True
            elif c.kind == "gun" and c.fire_flag:
                if shot_perp_error(muzzle, c.point, next_aim[0], next_aim[1]) <= c.tol:
                    fire[c.slot] = True

        return cmd_aim, fire, self._want_reload(weapons, in_combat=has_los)

    def _want_reload(self, weapons: list[Any], in_combat: bool) -> bool:
        for w in weapons:
            if w and w["kind"] == "gun":
                if w.get("reload", 0.0) > 0.0:
                    return False
                mag = w["stats"]["mag"]
                ammo = w.get("ammo", mag)
                if ammo == 0 or (not in_combat and ammo <= max(4, mag // 2)):
                    return True
        return False
```

### 3.2 3D Mortar/Laser Dodge & Rear-Armor Facing

```python
def compute_3d_dodge(
    obs: dict[str, Any],
    cfg: dict[str, Any],
) -> tuple[float, float, bool]:
    """
    Returns (dx, dy, urgent_boost) combining:
      1. Analytical 3D mortar landing-point escape (fixes D11)
      2. Charging enemy laser beam perpendicular sidestep
      3. Incoming gun projectile closest-approach dodge
    """
    me = obs["self"]
    mx, my, mz = me["pos"]
    st = me["stats"]
    my_r = cfg["mech"]["radius"] * (1.0 + cfg["cubes"]["size_per_cube"] * me["cubes"])
    g = cfg["weapons"]["mortar"]["gravity"]
    sr = cfg["weapons"]["mortar"]["splash_radius"]

    dx_tot, dy_tot = 0.0, 0.0
    urgent = False

    # 1. Projectiles (3D mortar impact + gun closest approach)
    for pr in obs.get("projectiles", ()):
        px, py, pz = pr["pos"]
        vx, vy, vz = pr["vel"]
        kind = pr["kind"]

        if kind == "mortar":
            dz = pz - mz
            disc = vz * vz + 2.0 * g * max(0.0, dz)
            if disc < 0.0:
                continue
            t_imp = (vz + math.sqrt(disc)) / g
            if t_imp <= 0.0 or t_imp > 3.5:
                continue
            ix, iy = px + vx * t_imp, py + vy * t_imp
            ox, oy = mx - ix, my - iy
            d = math.hypot(ox, oy)
            danger_r = sr + my_r + 3.5
            if d < danger_r:
                w = (1.0 - d / danger_r) * (2.5 / max(0.3, t_imp))
                if d < 1e-3:
                    # Fallback perpendicular to shell horizontal velocity
                    hv = math.hypot(vx, vy) or 1.0
                    ox, oy, d = -vy / hv, vx / hv, 1.0
                dx_tot += (ox / d) * w
                dy_tot += (oy / d) * w
                if t_imp < 1.1 and d < sr + my_r + 1.0:
                    urgent = True

        else:
            # Straight-line gun / NPC bullet 3D closest approach
            v2 = vx * vx + vy * vy + vz * vz
            if v2 < 1.0:
                continue
            rx, ry, rz = mx - px, my - py, (mz + 4.5) - pz
            t_ca = (rx * vx + ry * vy + rz * vz) / v2
            if t_ca < 0.0 or t_ca > 0.75:
                continue
            cx, cy, cz = rx - vx * t_ca, ry - vy * t_ca, rz - vz * t_ca
            if abs(cz) > 5.5:
                continue
            d2d = math.hypot(cx, cy)
            if d2d < my_r + 2.2:
                hv = math.hypot(vx, vy) or 1.0
                px_u, py_u = -vy / hv, vx / hv
                sign = 1.0 if (cx * px_u + cy * py_u) >= 0.0 else -1.0
                w = (1.0 - d2d / (my_r + 2.2)) * 1.1
                dx_tot += sign * px_u * w
                dy_tot += sign * py_u * w

    # 2. Charging enemy lasers: step perpendicular to their actual aim ray
    for m in obs.get("mechs", ()):
        if m.get("kind") != "mech" or not m.get("charging"):
            continue
        ex, ey, ez = m["pos"]
        eyaw = m["aim"][0]
        ax, ay = math.cos(eyaw), math.sin(eyaw)
        rx, ry = mx - ex, my - ey
        along = rx * ax + ry * ay
        if along < 0.0 or along > cfg["weapons"]["laser"]["range"]:
            continue
        perp = rx * (-ay) + ry * ax
        if abs(perp) < my_r + 4.0:
            sign = 1.0 if perp >= 0.0 else -1.0
            dx_tot += sign * (-ay) * 2.4
            dy_tot += sign * ax * 2.4
            urgent = True

    mag = math.hypot(dx_tot, dy_tot)
    if mag > 1e-4:
        return (dx_tot / mag, dy_tot / mag, urgent)
    return (0.0, 0.0, False)

```

### 3.3 Rooftop Index (Built at `hello`) & Jump-Jet Climb Controller

```python
@dataclass
class Rooftop:
    rid: int
    cx: float
    cy: float
    top_z: float
    climb_h: float
    entries: list[tuple[float, float, float, float]]  # (ground_x, ground_y, roof_x, roof_y)
    peeks: list[tuple[float, float, float, float]]    # (peek_x, peek_y, hide_x, hide_y)

class RooftopIndex:
    """Precomputes climbable building roofs from v2/world.json during the 20s hello budget."""
    def __init__(self, world_json: Optional[dict[str, Any]], world: Any, cfg: dict[str, Any]):
        self.roofs: list[Rooftop] = []
        if not world_json or world is None or not hasattr(world, "ground_z"):
            return
        max_climb = (cfg["mech"]["energy"] / cfg["mech"]["jet_drain"]) * cfg["mech"]["max_rise_speed"] * 0.72
        r_body = cfg["mech"]["radius"]

        for idx, p in enumerate(world_json.get("prisms", ())):
            if p.get("kind") != "building":
                continue
            ring = p.get("ring", ())
            if len(ring) < 6:
                continue
            xs = ring[0::2]
            ys = ring[1::2]
            minx, maxx, miny, maxy = min(xs), max(xs), min(ys), max(ys)
            if (maxx - minx) < 9.0 or (maxy - miny) < 9.0:
                continue
            cx, cy = 0.5 * (minx + maxx), 0.5 * (miny + maxy)
            top_z = float(p["top"])
            gz = world.ground_z(cx, cy)
            climb_h = top_z - gz
            if climb_h < 5.0 or climb_h > max_climb:
                continue

            entries = []
            peeks = []
            n = len(xs)
            for i in range(n):
                x0, y0 = xs[i], ys[i]
                x1, y1 = xs[(i + 1) % n], ys[(i + 1) % n]
                elen = math.hypot(x1 - x0, y1 - y0)
                if elen < 6.0:
                    continue
                mx, my = 0.5 * (x0 + x1), 0.5 * (y0 + y1)
                # Outward normal (test which side is lower)
                nx, ny = (y1 - y0) / elen, -(x1 - x0) / elen
                if world.top_z(mx + nx * 3.5, my + ny * 3.5) >= top_z - 1.0:
                    nx, ny = -nx, -ny
                gx, gy = mx + nx * (r_body + 2.0), my + ny * (r_body + 2.0)
                rx, ry = mx - nx * (r_body + 1.5), my - ny * (r_body + 1.5)
                hx, hy = mx - nx * (r_body + 5.0), my - ny * (r_body + 5.0)
                # Ensure clear vertical climb shaft (no eaves/bridges blocking jet)
                if (
                    world.top_z(gx, gy) < top_z - 3.5
                    and abs(world.top_z(rx, ry) - top_z) < 0.8
                    and not world.solid(gx, gy, top_z + 2.0)
                    and not world.solid(gx, gy, top_z + 6.0)
                ):
                    entries.append((gx, gy, rx, ry))
                    if abs(world.top_z(hx, hy) - top_z) < 0.8:
                        peeks.append((rx, ry, hx, hy))
            if entries:
                self.roofs.append(Rooftop(idx, cx, cy, top_z, climb_h, entries, peeks))

    def best_roof(
        self,
        pos: tuple[float, float, float],
        zone_center: tuple[float, float],
        zone_radius: float,
        max_dist: float,
        max_climb_h: float,
    ) -> Optional[Rooftop]:
        best, best_s = None, -1e9
        for rf in self.roofs:
            if rf.climb_h > max_climb_h:
                continue
            d_me = math.hypot(rf.cx - pos[0], rf.cy - pos[1])
            if d_me > max_dist:
                continue
            d_z = math.hypot(rf.cx - zone_center[0], rf.cy - zone_center[1])
            if d_z > max(10.0, zone_radius - 15.0):
                continue
            score = -d_me - 0.15 * d_z - 0.6 * rf.climb_h + 15.0 * min(4, len(rf.peeks))
            if score > best_s:
                best, best_s = rf, score
        return best


class RoofClimber:
    """Stateless-safe jump-jet controller to climb a precomputed building edge."""
    def __init__(self):
        self.roof: Optional[Rooftop] = None
        self.entry: Optional[tuple[float, float, float, float]] = None
        self.started_t: float = 0.0

    def start(self, roof: Rooftop, pos: tuple[float, float, float], t: float) -> None:
        self.roof = roof
        self.entry = min(roof.entries, key=lambda e: math.hypot(e[0] - pos[0], e[1] - pos[1]))
        self.started_t = t

    def clear(self) -> None:
        self.roof = None
        self.entry = None

    def step(self, obs: dict[str, Any]) -> tuple[list[float], bool, bool, str]:
        """Returns (move_xy, boost, jump, status: 'CLIMBING'|'DONE'|'ABORT')."""
        if self.roof is None or self.entry is None:
            return [0.0, 0.0], False, False, "ABORT"
        me = obs["self"]
        x, y, z = me["pos"]
        gx, gy, rx, ry = self.entry

        if z >= self.roof.top_z - 0.5 and math.hypot(rx - x, ry - y) < 4.5 and me["grounded"]:
            self.clear()
            return [0.0, 0.0], False, False, "DONE"
        if obs["t"] - self.started_t > 9.0 or (not me["grounded"] and me["energy"] < 4.0 and z < self.roof.top_z - 1.0):
            self.clear()
            return [0.0, 0.0], False, False, "ABORT"

        # Feet have cleared the roof edge -> move horizontally onto roof
        if z >= self.roof.top_z + 0.35:
            dx, dy = rx - x, ry - y
            d = math.hypot(dx, dy) or 1.0
            return [dx / d, dy / d], False, False, "CLIMBING"

        # Approach climb wall foot
        d_entry = math.hypot(gx - x, gy - y)
        if d_entry > 2.2 and me["grounded"]:
            dx, dy = gx - x, gy - y
            return [dx / d_entry, dy / d_entry], False, False, "CLIMBING"

        # At climb foot: jump when grounded, then jet straight up
        if me["grounded"]:
            return [0.0, 0.0], False, True, "CLIMBING"
        # Airborne: bias slightly away from wall until clear so we don't snag eaves
        dx, dy = gx - x, gy - y
        d = math.hypot(dx, dy)
        mv = [dx / d * 0.3, dy / d * 0.3] if d > 0.5 else [0.0, 0.0]
        return mv, True, False, "CLIMBING"

```

```python
def pick_upgrade(offer: Optional[dict[str, Any]], obs: dict[str, Any], cfg: dict[str, Any]) -> Optional[int]:
    if not offer or not offer.get("choices"):
        return None
    held = {w["kind"] for w in obs["self"]["weapons"] if w}
    best_i, best_s = 0, -1e9
    for i, ch in enumerate(offer["choices"]):
        uid = ch["id"]
        s = UPGRADE_WEIGHTS.get(uid, 20.0)
        # Boost weapon upgrades matching our actual secondary
        if uid.startswith("laser_") and "laser" in held:
            s += 15.0
        if uid.startswith("mortar_") and "mortar" in held and obs["zone"]["radius"] <= 160:
            s += 18.0
        # Nanites are exponentially valuable while alive early/mid
        if uid == "nanites" and obs["t"] < 260:
            s += 15.0
        if s > best_s:
            best_i, best_s = i, s
    return best_i


def maybe_take_weapon(obs: dict[str, Any], cfg: dict[str, Any]) -> tuple[Optional[int], Optional[int]]:
    """Returns (take_id, drop_slot) adhering strictly to protocol rules."""
    me = obs["self"]
    mx, my, mz = me["pos"]
    r_pick = cfg["loot"]["pickup_radius"]
    weapons = me["weapons"]
    held_kinds = {w["kind"] for w in weapons if w}
    late = obs["zone"]["radius"] <= 100.0
    rank = {"laser": 3, "mortar": 3 if late else 2, "lightning": 2 if late else 1, "gun": 0}

    # Never drop/swap a charging laser
    slot1_busy = bool(weapons[1] and weapons[1]["kind"] == "laser" and weapons[1].get("charge", 0.0) > 0.0)

    best = None
    best_val = -1
    for p in obs.get("pickups", ()):
        px, py, pz = p["pos"]
        if math.hypot(px - mx, py - my) > r_pick or abs(pz - mz) > 4.0:
            continue
        k = p["kind"]
        if k in held_kinds:
            continue
        val = rank.get(k, 0)
        if weapons[1] is None:
            if val > best_val:
                best, best_val = (p["id"], None), val
        elif not slot1_busy and val > rank.get(weapons[1]["kind"], 0):
            if val > best_val:
                best, best_val = (p["id"], 1), val
    return best if best is not None else (None, None)

```
